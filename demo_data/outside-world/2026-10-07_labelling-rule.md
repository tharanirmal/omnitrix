# New front-of-pack labelling rule for packaged snacks from 1 January 2027

**This article is fictional and exists only as demo data.**

The food regulator has notified an amendment requiring all packaged savoury snacks sold from
1 January 2027 to carry a front-of-pack nutrition panel showing salt, fat and sugar per 30 g
serving, in a minimum 8-point font. Existing printed stock may be sold until 31 March 2027.

Manufacturers are advised to finalise new artwork with their packaging suppliers by November to
avoid disruption, as most laminate suppliers need 8-10 weeks for new cylinders.
