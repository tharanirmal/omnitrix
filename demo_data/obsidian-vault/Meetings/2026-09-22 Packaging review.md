# Packaging review - 22 Sep

## Discussion
- Meera presented FreshWrap's offer: about 12% cheaper than GreenPack, but they have never supplied
  food-grade laminates at our volume and there is no quality track record with us.
- Priya: cash is tight until the Series A closes. Onboarding a new vendor means advance payments,
  new quality checks and a trial run.
- Arjun: a failed packaging trial during Diwali production would be very costly.

## Decision
**No new vendors this quarter (until 31 December).** We stay with GreenPack for the Diwali run and
all Q4 orders. Reason: protect cash flow and avoid onboarding risk before the Series A.
Rejected: switching the Diwali packs to FreshWrap. Revisit in the first week of January.
